\begin{abstract}
This is the \textbf{full English} position paper for review: uplift fulfillment, single-agent SoT, and \emph{multi-agent collaboration} patterns (ReAct chains, multi-hop RAG, self-consistency samples, pro/con/judge debate, LangGraph checkpoints, and stacked micro+macro levers).
For each scenario we state \textbf{where} \texttt{fit\_agent\_plan} / two-batch localization runs, objects $X,Y,Z,W$ (and $T$ in uplift), \textbf{incremental value} vs uniform baselines, and \textbf{attached tables} R1--R9 regenerated from JSON (nine collaboration runs including plan--execute and stacked debate levers).
Collaboration means multiple segments, roles, hops, or rounds sharing one FSDS attribution window---shift on one participant triggers budget reallocation for the team, not a blind global token cut.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Polished scenario narratives}

\paragraph{ReAct (\texttt{react\_observation\_drift}).}
Three segments collaborate sequentially; LIVE noise on observation embedding triggers extra checks/tier on obs without re-expanding thought---workflow-efficient collaboration.

\paragraph{RAG multihop (\texttt{rag\_multihop\_hop3\_drift}).}
Four hops collaborate; drift on reasoning hop after retrieve localizes budget to the failing collaboration edge (retrieve$\rightarrow$reason handoff).

\paragraph{Self-consistency (\texttt{self\_consistency\_chain\_dispersion}).}
Parallel chain embeddings collaborate via dispersion; FSDS reduces checks/tokens on stable chains; adaptive $N$ vignette (Table~\ref{tab:agent-collab} companion row) shows sample-count lever when dispersion falls.

\paragraph{Multi-agent debate (\texttt{multi\_agent\_debate\_con\_drift}).}
Pro, con, and judge share one plan; con drift under LIVE mimics adversarial channel shift; FSDS down-weights con tier while preserving judge budget on critical path.

\paragraph{Plan--execute (\texttt{plan\_execute\_midstep\_drift}).}
Five planner steps collaborate; drift on step~3 mimics a failing sub-task in a long horizon plan---FSDS adds budget/checks on the weak step without inflating the whole plan uniformly.

\paragraph{LangGraph (\texttt{langgraph\_tool\_call\_drift}).}
Five checkpoint nodes collaborate; tool\_call drift matches production hook---partial replay instead of full graph CAPEX relearn.

\paragraph{Stacked collaboration (\texttt{stacked\_multi\_agent\_fsds\_plus\_early\_stop}).}
\textbf{Micro:} per-role FSDS budgets (Table~\ref{tab:stacked-ma}).
\textbf{Macro:} \texttt{debate\_early\_stop\_round} on $(R,B,d)$ tensors stops when round centroids stabilize---agents ``agree'' across rounds, so further debate is OPEX waste.
